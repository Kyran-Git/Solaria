# Venus: The Greenhouse Planet

*Composition, interior, and atmospheric heat trapping*

**What you will learn:**
- Where Venus sits and how it compares to Earth
- What Venus is made of
- How its interior got layers
- Why Venus is hotter than Mercury

---

## 1. Meet the Planet

- Venus is the **2nd planet** from the Sun.
- It is the **6th largest** planet in the solar system.
- It is a **terrestrial planet** (an *inner* or *rocky* planet) with a **solid, rocky surface**, unlike the gas and ice giants.
- Like Earth and the rest of the solar system, Venus formed about **4.5 billion years ago**.

---

## 2. Composition and Interior

### Bulk Composition

Venus has almost the same bulk makeup as **Earth and Mars**.

| Part | Share of mass | Made of |
|---|---|---|
| **Metallic core material** | About **1/3 (33%)** | Iron-nickel or iron-sulfur |
| **Silicates** | About **2/3 (67%)** | Silicon + oxygen minerals |

```
  VENUS MASS DISTRIBUTION

  +--------------------------------------------+
  |  SILICATES (silicon + oxygen)       ~67%   |
  |                                            |
  |                                            |
  +--------------------------------------------+
  |  IRON-NICKEL / IRON-SULFUR METAL    ~33%   |
  +--------------------------------------------+
```

### Oxidized Chemistry

Inner solar system bodies like Venus are mostly **oxygen compounds** (such as the **silicate minerals** in their crust and mantle). This is **oxidized** chemistry, the opposite of the **reduced**, hydrogen-dominated chemistry of the outer solar system.

### Differentiation: How Venus Got Layers

1. **Heat it up.** Venus had to be heated above the melting point of rock, typically **above 1300 K**.
2. **Metal sinks.** Gravity pulled dense **iron-nickel and iron-sulfur** toward the center to form a **metallic core**.
3. **Silicates float.** Lighter silicates rose to form the **crust**.
4. **Cool and freeze.** The layered structure was preserved as the planet cooled.

### Diagram: Venus's Layers

```

  ==============================================

  |---------------------------------------------|     |

  |  iron-nickel / iron-sulfur                  |     v
```

## 3. Atmosphere and the Greenhouse Effect

### A Thick CO₂ Atmosphere

Venus has an **extraordinarily thick atmosphere** made mostly of **carbon dioxide (CO₂)**.

### Heat Trapping

- The dense CO₂ acts like a **powerful blanket**.
- It lets heat **build up at the surface** and **blocks it from escaping back to space**. This is the **greenhouse effect**.

### The Temperature Surprise

Normally, surface temperature **drops with the square root of distance** from the Sun. Mercury, the closest planet, reaches **280 °C to 430 °C** on its sunlit side.

```
  Distance from Sun:   Mercury (closest)   <   Venus (farther)
  Expected temperature: HOTTEST              cooler?
  What actually happens: hot                 HOTTER THAN MERCURY
                                             (CO2 blanket)
```

> **Big idea:** Atmosphere can matter more than distance. Venus is farther out than Mercury but has a hotter surface.

---

## 4. Surface and Environment

- **Solid crust:** Venus has a **solid silicate rock** surface.
- **Geological features:** Radar and geological observations study patterns on the surface, including **polygonal crack formations**. Scientists examine these for clues about how the surface evolved and about **historical surface fluid processes**, such as **possible former seafloors**.

## 5. The Terrestrial Planets Compared

| Feature | Mercury | **Venus** | Earth | Mars |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Distance rank** | 1st | **2nd** | 3rd | 4th |
| **Size rank** | Smallest | **6th largest** | 5th largest | 7th largest |
| **Surface** | Solid / rocky | **Solid / rocky** | Solid / rocky | Solid / rocky |
| **Bulk composition** | Highest metal fraction | **About 1/3 metal, 2/3 silicates** | About 1/3 metal, 2/3 silicates | About 1/3 metal, 2/3 silicates |
| **Atmosphere** | Almost none (exosphere) | **Thick CO₂** | Nitrogen / oxygen | Thin CO₂ |
| **Chemistry** | Oxidized | **Oxidized** | Oxidized | Oxidized |
| **Temperature** | 280 to 430 °C (sunlit) | **Hotter than Mercury (CO₂ insulation)** | Moderate (between water's freezing and boiling points) | Cold now, possibly temperate in the past |

## Key Terms

| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Terrestrial planet** | Rocky planet with a solid surface |
| **Silicate** | Mineral made of silicon and oxygen |
| **Oxidized chemistry** | Dominated by oxygen compounds |
| **Differentiation** | Sorting of a molten planet into layers by density |
| **Greenhouse effect** | Atmosphere trapping heat near the surface |
| **Insulation** | Something that slows heat escaping |
